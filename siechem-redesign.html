Why Choose Siechem

Specialty Engineering. Full Customization.

Since 2002, Siechem has been at the forefront of the wire and cable industry, designing and manufacturing over 22 million configurations. We combine technical design consultancy, E-Beam polymer modification, and vertically integrated compounding to build the exact wires your systems require.

Our team of applications engineers collaborates directly with clients to optimize weight, flexibility, electrical shield coverage, and environmental survivability for critical systems.

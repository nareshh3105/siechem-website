Research & Development

Govt. of India recognised R&D centre specialising in E-Beam crosslinking, polymer compounding, and new material formulations.

Siechem's in-house R&D is recognized by the Govt. of India, Ministry of Science & Technology — one of the most prestigious approvals the company has received. Siechem's R&D team, manned by well-experienced and young technocrats, is engaged in the development of more than 23 million part numbers of wires and cables covering 34 different segments, has registered many patents and published technical papers.

The team is continuously working on finding alternate materials which are environmentally friendly and on improving process optimization. As the market leader in the speciality wires and cables segment in India, Siechem's R&D team is engaged in the reduction of emission and pollution, and in customer-specific development of wires & cables to reduce weight, improve flexibility and enhance Oil, Chemical, UV, Ozone, Electrical, Mechanical, Thermal & Fire resistance properties — without compromising quality, environmental and safety standards.

Siechem spends about 5% of its revenue annually on R&D projects. Our R&D team has a handful of new projects, as always.
